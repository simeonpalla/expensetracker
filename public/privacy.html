Who we are

Personal OS (“the app”) is a personal expense tracker operated by [YOUR NAME / COMPANY] (“we”). Contact: [SUPPORT EMAIL].

What we collect

  • Account: your email address and a password (stored only as a salted hash by our authentication provider; we never see it).
  • Your financial entries: transactions you add (amount, date, category, payee, payment method label, notes), your categories, the names of your payment accounts/cards (never card or account numbers), and your settings (budgets, giving floor, salary account).
  • Technical data: standard server logs (request time, status, a random request ID) and, if an error occurs, an error report that contains the function name and request ID but not your entries.

We do not collect bank credentials, card numbers, contacts, location, or advertising identifiers, and we do not use third-party advertising or tracking scripts.

Bill scanning

When you scan a bill, the photo is read on your device. The image is not uploaded to our servers. Only the details you review and choose to save become a transaction.

How we use it

To run the app for you: show your data, compute your dashboard, budgets and forecasts, keep you signed in, and keep the service secure and working. We do not sell your data and do not use it for advertising.

Who processes it

  • Supabase — database and authentication.
  • Netlify — hosting and serverless functions.
  • Sentry — error monitoring (no entry contents).

Data may be stored in the regions those providers use for our project: [REGION].

Cookies

We use strictly necessary, HttpOnly cookies to keep you signed in. We use no analytics or advertising cookies.

Retention

Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Access is restricted per user by database row-level security. No system is perfectly secure; we will notify affected users and authorities of a breach as the law requires.
